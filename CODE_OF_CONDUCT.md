# Code of Conduct

Seatbit is made by and for people who like LAN parties. We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their age, background, identity, experience or skill.

## Our standards

Good:

- being friendly and patient, also with beginners;
- respecting different opinions and experiences;
- giving and taking constructive feedback gracefully;
- owning up to mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Not OK:

- insults, harassment, trolling, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances;
- publishing others' private information (such as addresses or emails) without their permission;
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately: send a private vulnerability report through the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/bitsandbricks-ch/seatbit/security) (it is private to maintainers, also for non-security reports), or write to a maintainer directly. All reports are handled confidentially.
