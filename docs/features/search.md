# Quick search

Jump straight to an event, a person or a clan without clicking through lists.

**Who:** everyone signed in · **Where:** the magnifier in the top bar, or Ctrl+K / ⌘K

- Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or tap the magnifier in the header.
- Type part of a name, a nick, an event or a place. Results are grouped into *Events* (upcoming first, then past ones), *People* and *Clans*.
- Move with the arrow keys and press **Enter**, or tap a result. **Esc** closes the search.

Search only looks at what you can already see: the events, members and clans of your own clans. Drafts show up only for the people who organizer them. People appear the way their clan shows names, and a person opens on their clan profile. On the clan pages, that profile opens in the side panel.
